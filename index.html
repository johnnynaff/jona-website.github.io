Elena Varga

Associate Professor, Department of Mathematics, University of Example

I study the representation theory of finite groups and algebras, and how local structure, such as Sylow subgroups and blocks, controls global behaviour.

Theorem (Sylow). Let $G$ be a finite group of order $p^a m$, where $p$ is a prime not dividing $m$. Then for every $0 \le k \le a$, $G$ has a subgroup of order $p^k$.

Show proof

Proof (Wielandt). Let $\Omega$ be the set of subsets of $G$ of size $p^k$. The $p$-adic valuation of $|\Omega| = \binom{p^a m}{p^k}$ is $a-k$, so some $G$-orbit under left multiplication has size with valuation at most $a-k$. If $H$ is the stabiliser of a set $S$ in that orbit, then $|G|/|H|$ has valuation at most $a-k$, so $p^k$ divides $|H|$. But $S$ contains the coset $Hs$ for any $s \in S$, so $|H| \le |S| = p^k$. Hence $|H| = p^k$. ∎

About

I am an Associate Professor at the University of Example. Before that I was a postdoctoral researcher in Utrecht and a Junior Research Fellow in Cambridge. I completed my PhD in 2016 under the supervision of A. Advisor.

Most of my work sits between group theory, homological algebra and computation. I enjoy problems where a small calculation in a specific group suggests a statement that turns out to hold in general.

Research

Blocks and local–global conjectures

Many conjectures in modular representation theory say that the representations of a group $G$ in characteristic $p$ are determined by those of its $p$-local subgroups. I work on cases where the defect group is small enough to allow explicit derived equivalences.

Derived equivalences and categorification

I am interested in when two algebras have equivalent derived categories, and in what invariants survive. Recent work with J. Okafor uses tilting complexes to compare blocks over $p$-adic rings.

Computational methods

I write software in GAP to compute character tables and decomposition matrices, and use it to test conjectures before trying to prove them. Code accompanying my papers is on GitHub.
