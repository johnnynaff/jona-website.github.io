Research

Stochastic Partial Differential Equations (SPDEs) and Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs).

Numerical analysis

Design of suitable numerical methods for approximating the solutions to certain classes of SPDEs and SDEs. This is a particular challenge when working with inputs that verify poor regularity assumptions. In order to deal with them one has to exploit the regularization properties of the noise under consideration.

Long-time behaviour

I have worked on the long-time analysis of SDEs driven by fractional Brownian motion, both theoretically and numerically.

Regularization by noise

Fractional noise

I write software in GAP to compute character tables and decomposition matrices, and use it to test conjectures before trying to prove them. Code accompanying my papers is on GitHub.
